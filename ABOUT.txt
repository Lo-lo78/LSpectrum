LSpectrum 4.5dB Analyzer
Version: 0.99.1
Release date: 18 November 2026
Author: Lo-lo
Repository: https://github.com/Lo-lo78/LSpectrum

Accessible 32-band stereo spectrum analyzer VST3 for REAPER.
Reference: 4.5 dB per octave, near 1 kHz.
Separate Left/Right differences, non-silent block averaging,
and 36 dB/octave bandpass audition on each flank.

Ctrl+Up/Down bandpass gain (-120 to +24 dB), default 0 dB.
Countdown 1 to 600 seconds, default 10 seconds.
Reference noise; no sine-wave oscillator.

Settings: %APPDATA%\Lo-lo\LSpectrum 4.5dB Analyzer\
